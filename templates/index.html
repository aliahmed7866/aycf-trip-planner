How this scan works

  1. Load the newest parsed Wizz AYCF availability snapshot.
  2. Build direct and valid one-stop candidates from those routes.
  3. Check each required leg against your authenticated Multipass session.
  4. Only show combinations where every live leg is available.

Scan safety

  • Live lookups are cached briefly across scans.
  • Requests are sequential and throttled.
  • Transient Wizz errors get one conservative retry.
  • Direct routes are considered before one-stop routes.
  • Your Wizz password is never stored by this app.
