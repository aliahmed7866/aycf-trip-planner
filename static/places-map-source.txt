World map and country/territory catalogue derived from Natural Earth.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
Map: geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Catalogue: geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson plus 110m features
Retrieved: 2026-09-06
Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
The bundled map uses an equirectangular projection with rounded coordinates.
Countries and territories are counted together; these are not sovereign-state totals.
Small islands may only be available in the country selector. Antarctica is in the
selector but omitted from the map viewport. Boundaries follow the source dataset.
